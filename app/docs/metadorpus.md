## Data model

One row is one dot. The inspectors treat every facet as something that can filter, group, or place that dot. Facets on screen: what it is, date, season, hour of the day, class, color, artist, range, length, method, research source, wellbeing and money, ICAR Raven audit, notes and depth, articles. A new question is a new recipe over that table, not a new chart type and not a new query shape.

## Chart patterns

- The default is a loose point cloud. Changing the recipe moves the same points.
- Hour of the day becomes a ring. A cyclic facet is drawn as a cycle.
- A date span (August 2021 through November 2024) becomes a ridgeline of density spikes, not bars.
- Two labeled masses sit apart, Metadorpus and cortex, with a small labeled remainder, failure in survival, between them.
- A tree layout is a trunk of points plus a dense wedge.
- Group by "what it is" and order "internal in order" packs the points into a disk.
- The right rail is the recipe: Anatomy, Layout, Order, Option first, then Group by, Sort, and a speed for the move between layouts.
- The bottom strip is a count ledger (method, source, money, audit, notes, articles), not a color legend.
- Marks stay ink on a pale field. Labels sit on the clusters.
